Fix sign-in popups and tag casing

Two bugs that made the app look broken.

The preload required hwid.cjs, which uses node:crypto and node:child_process.
In a sandboxed preload those are unavailable, so the require threw,
contextBridge never ran, window.rift stayed undefined, and bridgeAvailable()
returned false. That is why the app reported "browser preview" instead of
talking to its own engine. The require was also pointless: hwid is only ever
used in the main process.

Firebase OAuth needs a real popup window. setWindowOpenHandler denied every
window.open, so sign-in could never start and the only error visible was
"the sign-in window was blocked". Known provider hosts are now allowed into a
locked-down child window - no node, no preload, no nested popups - and it
closes itself once the provider redirects back.

The gate also waited on authRequired being set by a fire-and-forget import,
so it could stay false and let the console through. It now waits for the auth
check to settle, and if firebase cannot load at all the app stays usable
rather than locking the user out of their own machine.

Tags were parsed with a lowercase-only v strip, so a release published as
V0.2.1 kept its letter and degraded to NaN. Tags now normalise across v, V,
no prefix, and a release- prefix.
